How to play
  • Destroy enemy towers to earn crowns. Destroying the King Tower is an instant three-crown win.
  • The King Tower sleeps (💤) until something damages it — then its cannon joins the fight for the rest of the match.
  • Elixir regenerates 1 every 2.8s up to 10. Double in the final minute, triple in overtime.
  • 3 minutes of regulation. If crowns are tied, up to 2 minutes of sudden-death overtime: first tower down wins. Still tied? The side whose weakest tower has the lowest HP loses.
  • Click a card then the arena (or drag it). Keys 1–4 pick cards, A fires your Hero's ability, Esc cancels.
  • Cheap defenses against expensive pushes win elixir trades — watch your trade counter.
